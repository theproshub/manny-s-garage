# References — not shipped

Working material. Nothing in this folder is served, imported, or deployed.
It sits outside `public/` deliberately: anything under `public/` is reachable
by URL on mannygarage.com whether or not the code links to it.

## `job-photos/`

Retailer product-listing photographs (bunk beds, vanities, TV stands, curtain
rods, projector screens, LED strip lighting) collected as references for the
kinds of jobs the Handyman schedule prices. They were sitting in
`public/images/` — unreferenced by any component, but publicly downloadable at
`mannygarage.com/images/…` on every deploy, along with 56MB of build weight.

**These are third-party copyrighted product photos. They are reference only.**
Do not put them on the site. If a page needs a picture of a job, it needs a
photograph of *this shop's* work, or a licensed image.

## `generated-service-photos/`

The fourteen generated photographs that used to run in the home page slideshow,
kept because the prompts and the review notes in `docs/service-photography.md`
refer to them.

They came out because several betrayed themselves as generated. `oil-change`
carried a bottle label of invented letterforms — a blue "ZΠOUZ" over paragraphs
of fake text — and showed oil being poured upward from under a lifted car.
`check-engine` showed a scanner displaying a gibberish paragraph over a
meaningless graph, every key on its keypad marked with a nonsense glyph.
`blinds` had no blind anywhere in the frame despite its alt text promising one.

The slideshow now runs real Unsplash photographs from `public/hero/`. If these
are regenerated to the standard in `docs/service-photography.md` — and checked
by cropping into every label, screen and printed surface at full resolution —
they can go back. Until then they are not fit to publish.
